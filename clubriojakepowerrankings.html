Club Rio Jake — Power Rankings

Actual record, total points, and the all-play record — what you'd have gone if you had played all eleven opponents every single week. Averaged, that's the power rank. The gap between all-play and actual is the schedule luck.

Notes

  • All-play compares your score to all eleven other scores each week, so a 14-week season is 154 all-play games. It strips out who the schedule happened to pair you with.
  • Regular season only. Playoff weeks are excluded on both sides, so points totals here are lower than the season totals Yahoo and CBS display.
  • 2011–2025 comes from Yahoo's weekly scoreboards. 2001–2010 comes from the CBS scoreboards archived in Evernote; those years count every week in which all twelve teams played.
  • Ties count as neither a win nor a loss on both sides of the comparison.
